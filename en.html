News

  • 2026.09
    Release
    Released darksec

    A GPU implementation of Dark sectioning (Cao et al., Nature Methods 2025) for removing out-of-focus background from widefield images. The algorithm is the published one: darksec reproduces its output and runs about 30× faster, and replaces the per-image normalisation with a single calibration per dataset and channel, so that every image in an experiment receives the same transform.

Research

We use cutting-edge fluorescence imaging to understand life through its spatiotemporal dynamics.

Our core themes are new epigenome-editing technologies, the regulation of genomic imprinting, and how 3D genome architecture controls transcription.

Dokkyo Medical University sits in a quiet, green setting well suited to focused research. A well-equipped core facility, the resources of a medical school and strong research support make it a good place to build a career. If you want to open up science from your own angle, get in touch.
